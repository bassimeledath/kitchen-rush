# Kitchen Rush v2 — checkpoints 1 and 2

**Useful work. Limited budgets. Unfamiliar kitchens.**

| Checkpoint | Delivered |
|---|---|
| **1 — Implement and validate** | Shared single-action protocol, generated recipe cards, paused inference clock, dollar/token allowances, quality and B80. **240 generated kitchens solved and replayed; 88 tests passed.** Full roster preserved: **30 configurations**, including Jev, after two aliases. |
| **2 — Pilot and audit** | Corrected canary, 18-episode smoke, **72-episode fresh pilot**. Every pilot request and replay verified. Three replacement graphs and matched model replays are ready for review. |

**Accounted cost: $10.5326 / $50.** This includes $10.5138 in usage-derived charges and
$0.0187 retained for an abandoned canary timeout whose actual bill is unknown. No overruns.

**B80:** GPT-5.4 Mini (low) reaches 80 at **8,192 tokens**; its conservative B80 is not reached.
Sonnet 4.6 (thinking off) does not reach 80 within tested budgets. Jev is N/A for B80 and
is included in the common dollar view. No token runs were dollar-censored. These are pilot findings, not stable rankings.

**Before the full run:**

- Review the conservative reservation policy. Sonnet sometimes stops with money and a
  finished plate remaining because another request cannot be safely reserved. This affects
  the dollar curves; it is not a model decision to stop.
- Freeze full-roster coverage and allocation; resolve PhoneLLM's missing dollar attribution.
  All existing models remain in scope. More independent kitchens are needed for release.
- Keep the generalization claim bounded: the fixed-card lookup baseline failed;
  the public, finite generator is not memorization-proof.

**Checkpoint 3 has not started.** It includes the full model rerun, README rewrite from
scratch, final graphs, replay presentation and rebrand. No v1 scores become v2 scores.
